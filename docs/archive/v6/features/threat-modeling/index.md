# Threat Modeling

Threat Modeling in HunterX is a systematic process for identifying, enumerating, and prioritizing potential threats and vulnerabilities in a target environment.

## Purpose

To understand the threat landscape, anticipate attacker moves, and prioritize defensive measures based on risk.

## Architecture

Employs methodologies like STRIDE, PASTA, or attack trees to model threats and generate attack graphs.

## Workflow

1. Asset identification and decomposition
2. Threat identification using threat intelligence and vulnerability data
3. Attack vector enumeration
4. Risk scoring and prioritization
5. Generation of mitigation recommendations

## Advantages

- Provides a structured approach to risk assessment
- Helps allocate resources to the most critical threats
- Produces actionable insights for remediation

## Integration

Outputs feed into the Knowledge Graph and are used by the Reasoning Engine to predict attack paths.

## Related Components

- Knowledge Graph (stores threat models and relationships)
- Reasoning Engine (simulates attack scenarios)
- Payload Intelligence (selects payloads based on attack paths)
